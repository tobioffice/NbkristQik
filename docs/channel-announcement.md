# 🤖 BOT UPGRADED - What's New

Been working on the bot these past days. Here's what changed:

Check attendance, tap "Bunk Plan" and it tells you exactly how many classes you can skip per subject while staying above 75%. Also shows which subjects need attention.

**🔍 Find yourself on the Leaderboard**
Open the leaderboard from the bot and your row shows up highlighted with your rank. No more scrolling through thousands of names.

**📡 Live Status Page**
New page showing bot uptime with 90-day history: checker.tobioffice.dev/status

**🛠️ Smarter errors**
Typos in roll numbers now get corrected - the bot suggests similar roll numbers instead of just failing. And if the college server acts up, the bot retries before giving up.

**⚡ Faster responses**
Sections get cached in the background now, so first-time checks are way quicker.
